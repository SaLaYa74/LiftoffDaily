Where did its partner go? 🤯

Arp 263 (NGC 3239) is an irregular galaxy in Leo, about 25 million light-years away, believed to be the result of a past galactic merger.
Its former companion is nowhere to be found: it may have been a dwarf galaxy that fully dissolved into Arp 263, or one too faint to spot.
Blue shows hydrogen gas lit by young stars where new stars form. The big bright star, BD+17 2217, is in our own galaxy, right in front.
