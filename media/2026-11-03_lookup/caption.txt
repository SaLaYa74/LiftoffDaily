The best meteor shower of 2026 is 6 weeks away 🌠

Geminids: peak night Dec 13-14. Up to 150 meteors an hour in a perfectly dark sky (real-world counts are lower, but still a show).
Best time: from 10pm, best around 2am. Where to look: anywhere overhead; they fan out from Gemini, high in the east.
Moon: 19% lit, sets around 9pm in the central US.
Wait, what? They come from an asteroid, 3200 Phaethon, not a comet.
