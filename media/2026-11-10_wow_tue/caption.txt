This treasure chest is full of stars 🤯

The "Treasure Chest" is a cometary globule in the Carina Nebula, about 7,500 light-years away: a cloud of gas and dust with a dense head and a sweeping tail.
Inside is a compact cluster of about 70 young stars. The most massive is a rare O-type star roughly 19 times the mass of the Sun, and the cluster is likely around 1.3 million years old.
Its shape was carved mostly by radiation and winds from massive stars nearby, including Eta Carinae, a system with a star about 5 million times as luminous as the Sun.
