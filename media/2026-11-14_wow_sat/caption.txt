Gravity is copying these galaxies 🤯

The golden galaxies are the cluster MACS J0454.1-0300, seen as it was when the Universe was about 8 billion years old. The orange ones are much farther away, behind it.
The cluster's huge mass bends and magnifies light from the galaxies behind it (gravitational lensing), stretching them into arcs. Some small clumps appear magnified more than 300 times.
Rarely, lensing shows the same galaxy four or more times. Researchers spotted that twice in this cluster, the first time it has been seen more than once in the same galaxy cluster.
